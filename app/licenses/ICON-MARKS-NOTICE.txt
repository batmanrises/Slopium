Clean icon marks
================
The monochrome marks in icons/marks/ are taken from the CleanFrame project
(https://github.com/nthnclrk/CleanFrame, MIT licence, see CleanFrame-MIT.txt) and its
webOS port. Per CleanFrame's notices:

 - Simple Icons service marks: CC0 (SimpleIcons-CC0.txt).
 - Disney+, ABC and SBS marks: Custom Brand Icons, MIT (CustomBrandIcons-MIT.txt).
 - Utility outlines (home, apps, art, settings, devices): original CleanFrame drawings, MIT.
 - Exact revisions and adaptations: CleanFrame-icon-sources.json.

Brand marks identify their services only. Their owners keep all trademark rights; this
launcher claims no endorsement or affiliation.
